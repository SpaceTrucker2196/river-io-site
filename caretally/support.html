Help

A person reads every email: support@river.io

Does CareTally replace my agency's app?

No. Keep clocking in with the app your agency or fiscal agent gave you. CareTally keeps your own record beside it and sends nothing to them.

Where is my data?

On your phone, and nowhere else. There is no account. If you get a new phone, your iPhone backup carries CareTally's data across with your other apps.

I'm on the free plan. Did I lose my old records?

No. The free plan shows the last 14 days, but nothing older is deleted. Start Pro and it all appears again.

How do I make a Care Summary?

Reports → Prepare for assessment. Pick the person and the dates, check how complete the record is, then tap Make the Care Summary.

How do I cancel Pro?

In your iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then CareTally. Apple handles the subscription; we cannot cancel it for you.

The hour limit looks wrong for my program.

Tell us. CareTally's limits come from the state's own rules, but rules change. Your agency and support coordinator have the final word. Email us with your program and what you were told.

Report a problem

support@river.io. Please do not send names or care details of the person you care for — describe the problem, not the record.
