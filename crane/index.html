One honest loop

The whole product is a single loop a charge nurse can explain — with a hard gate at both doors.

Post

Four fields. Requirements derive from your accreditation — you see exactly what the shift will demand before you post it.

Broadcast

Every panel agency gets the shift in one transaction at one timestamp — or rung by rung on your tier ladder, each rung atomic.

Bid

Agencies submit candidates blind to each other. Recruiters see a ranked roster with every point of the match explained.

Gate

Non-compliant workers are hard-blocked at submission and re-checked at award. No override, no per-tenant switch.

Place & pay

Award, geofenced time, invoice and vendor pay from the graph path — exact integer cents, traceable to the edges they came from.

Four things Crane guarantees

Not policies — invariants. Each one is enforced by code and proven by a test that ships with the product.

Vendor-neutral by construction

A broadcast writes every agency's edge in one transaction with one identical timestamp. Neutrality isn't asserted — it's a query any auditor can run, per tier.

The gate hard-blocks

Requirements derive from your accreditation, state, role, and unit. A worker missing any of them — or on a sanctions list — cannot be submitted or awarded. Incumbents alert; Crane enforces.

Money math is exact

Integer cents, computed from the graph money path, never estimated or silently rounded. Every invoice, vendor payment, fee, and cancellation guarantee traces to the edges it came from.

PHI never touches the marketplace

Documents, license numbers — even names — live in an encrypted vault, decryptable only along a valid authorization edge. The graph holds relationships and opaque references. Provable by scan.

The receipts, not just the alerts

Credentialing platforms tell you something expired. Crane is the enforcement layer — and it keeps the evidence.

Gate-decision trail

Every allow and block, on record

Each gate evaluation — including blocked attempts that never became submissions — leaves a decision with the reason and timestamp. Export any survey window as CSV. Nobody else sells the decision record.

Continuous monitoring

Truth doesn't go stale

Primary-source re-verification on a cadence; exclusion feeds and public-list screening that flag candidates for human review — a confirmed hit blocks instantly, a name match alone never does.

Self-healing market

A pulled shift reposts itself

Cancellation marks the record, reposts to your whole panel at one instant, demands no less than the original order — and pays the worker's contract guarantee when you cancelled late.

Why a graph

Staffing is a cross-tenant routing and fairness network — who may see whom, who reached whom first, which path the money took. Those are graph questions. Crane keeps them as edges in Neo4j, so neutrality, eligibility, authorization, and money are all answered the same way: by traversal, provably, one hop from the data they came from.
