A quiet window, and what labs say about human oversight

No new dark factory finding was confirmed in early November. Two secondary reports show labs and banks keeping humans in the loop.

The window from 3 to 5 November 2026 was quiet. This author found no new framework, spec or security incident from a primary source. Three items did appear in secondary news. This post reports them with their limits.

Anthropic keeps a human in the loop (reported 2 November)

TechCrunch reports on a regulatory filing from Anthropic. The filing says Anthropic "may soon reach its internal threshold for dramatically accelerated AI-assisted research." The report also says Anthropic does not expect to let Claude build successive frontier models without human supervision.

This author did not open the filing. The claim comes from TechCrunch only. It is a direction signal. The lesson for a small factory is clear: a lab with strong agents still gates its highest-stakes loop with a human.

Goldman Sachs limits coding tools (reported 3 November)

The Decoder reports that Goldman Sachs limited Claude Code and other coding tools to engineers. The reported reasons are productivity and risk management. This author found no primary source, so the item stays a report.

One cost figure (2 November)

TechCrunch reports that the Y Combinator CEO spends about $100,000 to $200,000 a year on AI tokens, mostly for coding agents. This is one person's figure. It does not show a typical cost.

What it means for the River.io Software Factory

  1. Keep CareTime, TimeForCare and MaterialsAndPractices at human-gated autonomy. They handle regulated data.
  2. Scope each agent to one repo and one GitHub issue.
  3. Set a token cap on each issue. Stop the loop at the cap.
  4. Add no MCP connectors or marketplace plugins. The factory keeps zero third-party dependencies.
  5. Run autonomous-loop tests in MeowPassword, weathergalactic and sloth. These repos carry low stakes.

Not featured

Products announced on 4 November, such as TestGrid ONE and Sysdig AI Defense, are agent tools, not dark factory findings. The OpenAI rogue-agent items from 26 and 30 October stay unconfirmed. This author could not open a primary page for them.
