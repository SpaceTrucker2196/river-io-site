The Heritage Line

Beef Supper. Since 1927.

Before the fleet, before the stations, before anyone thought to put a trading floor in orbit, there was a red label with an orange cat on it and a promise in two languages: the galaxy's deliciousness, every day, with plenty of beef and gravy in the recipe. The company that grew out of that label now spans the charted lanes. The label has not changed, because the cat on it has seniority.

Beef Supper remains complete nutrition for adult cats, board approved, nutrition council certified, and stocked at every station we serve. When a hauler talks about flying the heritage run, this is the pallet they mean.

Orbital Manufacturing

Made on Jupiter. Announced loudly.

When the Jupiter facility shipped its first full line, the marketing department was given one instruction: be dignified. This marquee is what came back. Eight hundred eighty-eight thousand, eight hundred eighty-eight servings on the counter, the whole catalog in lights, and a studio audience of production staff who had not slept and did not care.

The dignity review concluded that the numbers were accurate, the cans were real, and the audience was genuinely that happy. The marquee stayed. It has been re-lit for every million-serving milestone since, and the storm bands photograph better every year.

Standards & Certifications

Rules the whole galaxy can read.

Zero-G certification means the can behaves in freefall: the contents settle, the gravy holds, and the lid opens toward the diner rather than at them. The astronaut in the poster performed the qualification himself and insisted on the thumbs up.

Ordinance 12 is the shortest rule in the company handbook and the least argued. A cucumber startles a working cat, a startled cat startles a dock, and a startled dock costs money. The sign is posted at every Tuna Prime entrance, and yes, the incident it commemorates is documented, and no, we do not discuss it in print.

Quality Assurance

Every batch is tasted by management.

U.C.F. quality assurance is conducted by feline staff on the production floor. There are no forms. Approval is indicated by eating. Rejection is indicated by a look, and the batch is reformulated without discussion.

The humans asked how we maintain a zero-defect record. We told them: we simply refuse to eat defects. They wrote this down. Feline Quality Assurance · Production Floor 2
