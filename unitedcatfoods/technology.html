Energy Program

Compact fusion: the key to the cosmos.

U.C.F. fusion reactors are built to withstand the rigors of space travel, providing reliable power for long-duration missions. Clean, near-limitless energy opens new possibilities for research, exploration, and colonization while reducing the ecological footprint of deep-space operations.

Every Loaf-class freighter and every orbital café runs on U.C.F. fusion. The floors are warm because the physics is sound.

PrincipleMimics the sun: nuclear fusion in a compact, efficient, space-hardened vessel.
OutputNear-limitless clean energy for stations, fleets, and long-duration missions.
DeploymentHeart of the merchant fleet and the café network. Uptime is total. We nap near the reactor housing, which says everything.

Water Division

Harvesting comet ice. Delivering life.

The certified result →

Every bowl of water in the network started as the tail of a comet. The harvester approaches, scans, anchors, grapples, and refines, five verbs that took forty years to make routine. Its paired mechanical paws secure and fracture the ice before feeding it into the onboard crusher. The ice comes up at 90.7 percent water by mass, and the refinery does not let go of it until the impurities are out and the number reads 99.9.

The board below is the crew's own process placard, cut into its panels. It hangs in every harvester's mess, next to the sign about not naming the comets, which is ignored.

  1. 01Approach & ScanSurvey composition and structure.
  2. 02Anchor & SecureBrace the harvester against the comet.
  3. 03Grapple & ExtractBreak and lift ice with paired paws.
  4. 04Refine & FilterRemove impurities to 99.9% purity.
  5. 05Transport & SupplySeal and deliver to station reservoirs.
FeedstockTypical comet ice arrives at 90.7% water by mass.
PurityMulti-stage filtration delivers 99.9% certified water.
HandlingPaired paw grapples secure, fracture, and feed each load.

What comes out the far end is clear, stable, and station-ready. Every delivery is checked for purity, mineral balance, sealed transfer integrity, and habitat safety before it enters the distribution manifold.
