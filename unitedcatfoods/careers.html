Three species. One payroll.

United Cat Foods hires humans, intelligent cats, and thinking machines across the station network. The work is real, the floors are fusion-warmed, and the management has excellent night vision.

Workforce Doctrine

At United Cat Foods, we believe that embracing diversity in all its forms is key to innovation: a workplace where every individual, whether human, cat, or thinking machine, can thrive and contribute to our collective success. Diversity & Inclusion Initiative

Benefits

What we provide.

Staff access to the full Galactic Gourmet line. Quality assurance is technically everyone's job.

Education support

Tuition assistance for humans studying feline management. The curriculum is humbling and worthwhile.

Hiring Process

Four steps. No surprises.

Application review. Every submission is read. Response times vary with the afternoon sun.

Structured interview. A calm conversation about the work. You will be observed from a high shelf. This is normal.

Trial shift. Paid, supervised, and scored on competence and composure. Composure counts double.

Offer. Decisions are final. Cats do not renegotiate, though they may reconsider unprompted, later, on their own terms.
