# United Cat Foods image audit

Last reviewed: 2026-08-11

## Scope

- Reviewed every raster referenced by the 36 public HTML pages and the shared stylesheets.
- 112 referenced assets were reviewed in the initial pass.
- 98 clean, complete assets remain in public use after consolidation.
- Verified that every public image path resolves.

## Review criteria

- No partial headings, chopped footer copy, or neighboring-panel fragments.
- Text-bearing broadcast boards remain visible edge to edge.
- Page heroes and archive cards do not use destructive `cover` crops for text-bearing art.
- The official `logo.png` is the only site-chrome brand mark.
- Primary universe imagery keeps normal feline anatomy and the established industrial Showa-broadcast treatment.
- Captions describe the complete plate actually shown.

## Retired fragments

Fourteen overly tight exports were removed from public use and preserved under
`wiki/art/_quarantine/fragments/`. Public pages now use intact master boards or
complete standalone plates in their place.

Affected groups:

- Brachistochrone route-board fragments
- Tuna Prime overview, route, promise, and vault fragments
- Market movers fragment
- Loaf-class partial engineering crops
- Jupiter Ice refinery partial crop
- Comet miner composite fragment

## Display rules

Text-bearing page heroes, station cards, wiki plates, galleries, schematics,
and infobox images use contained framing on a dark industrial mat. Photography
and the text-free home-page hero may still use a full-bleed crop.
