The Posting

Life at the dock.

Enceladus throws its ocean into space on a schedule, and the Draw is the company facility polite enough to catch it. Harvest frames ride the south polar plumes and take food grade ice directly out of the sky, the only harvest operation on the network with zero excavation and, the crew notes, zero complaints from the moon.

The subsurface fisheries are the quieter marvel: trawler grade fishmeal drawn from vent communities through bore wells that the station's founding engineer described as fishing through the planet's own keyhole. The description was deemed unprofessional and accurate, and is now on the patch.

Freight out of the Draw is heavy, cold, and constant. Ice runs to the canneries and the cafes; fishmeal feeds the Row. Pilots who like their loads simple and their vistas absurd request this route by name.
