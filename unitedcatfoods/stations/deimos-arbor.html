The Posting

Life at the dock.

Deimos grows the other catnip. Where the Hollow's arbor chases intensity, the Deimos lot cultivates for calm: slower beds, longer cures, a concentrate the cafes buy for the afternoon service. The arbor cats hold strong opinions about this distinction and will share them if you dock long enough.

The clay pit is the quiet second income. Bonded absorbent clay from Deimos lines holds across the network, and the pit has run continuously since the station opened without once making the newsletter, which the pit crew considers the highest possible praise.

For freight purposes Deimos is a gentle origin with steady postings and the shortest deadhead on the Mars system run. Load clay, top up on grain, and enjoy the only dock in the system where the approach lights are dimmed for ambiance.
