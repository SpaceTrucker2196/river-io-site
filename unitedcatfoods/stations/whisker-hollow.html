The Posting

Life at the dock.

Whisker Hollow is the breadbasket. The terrace farms run down the flank of a shield volcano in stepped greenhouses, and the grain they ship moves more tonnage than any other single commodity on the network. When the Hollow has a surplus, every cannery in the system smells it within a day, and the load board turns gold.

The catnip arbor is the other business, smaller in tonnage and larger in reputation. Arbor Lot concentrate from Mars sets the benchmark grade, and the annual first pressing is escorted freight, not because anyone would steal it but because everyone would visit it.

Charter pilots know the Hollow as a reliable origin: deep stock, honest posting, and a dispatch office that words its contracts plainly. Bring ration goods in, haul the harvest out, and mind the dust season.
