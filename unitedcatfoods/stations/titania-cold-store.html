The Posting

Life at the dock.

Titania Cold Store is the network's deep archive. In the Uranus system the cold is not manufactured, only organized, and the Store's vaults age bluefin reserve under conditions no inner system cellar can counterfeit. Cellar sealed at Titania is the top of the label hierarchy and the auction floor at Tuna Prime treats it accordingly.

The station buys enormous quantities of everything a distant outpost needs and produces almost nothing but patience. Its standing ration orders are among the largest single postings on the board, which is why the longest legs in the freight network end here, and why they pay the way they do.

Crews rotate through Titania on long contracts and return quieter, in the good sense. The Store's unofficial motto is carved over the vault lift: nothing is lost that is kept properly.
