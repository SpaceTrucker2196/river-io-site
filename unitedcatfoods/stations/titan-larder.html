The Posting

Life at the dock.

Titan Larder is the outer system's pantry. The terrace domes grow a hardy cold cycle grain under sodium light, and the station's namesake vaults hold reserve stock calculated to feed the Saturn system through any conceivable interruption plus, per the station charter, one inconceivable one.

The Larder posts steadily and buys reliably, which makes it a favorite mid-route call for pilots working the long Saturn legs. Its catnip beds are small but the altitude, the crews insist, does something to the terpenes.

Station culture is built around the reserve inventory the way port towns are built around lighthouses. The vault ledger is hand initialed daily by the stationmaster, a practice that predates every automation the company has offered to install and will outlast the next several.
