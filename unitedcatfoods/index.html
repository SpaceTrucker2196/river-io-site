The Fleet

The Loaf-class heavy freighter.

Flagship of the U.C.F. merchant fleet, the Loaf-class hauler moves ninety-six thousand cubic meters of provisions per crossing. The hull geometry was selected after exhaustive study: compact, stable, and radiating quiet confidence. Mackerel-pattern plating provides thermal regulation. The tail section houses the communications array and is repositioned when the ship is thinking.

Every Loaf-class vessel is fusion-powered, crewed by all three workforce species, and warm on the inside. Docking requests are answered when convenient.
