The Markets

The Tuna Prime Exchange, open outcry, closed claws.

The galaxy's premier protein commodity market runs on open outcry because the traders prefer it, and the traders are cats, so the matter is settled. Spot tuna at twelve thousand eight hundred fifty credits the ton; futures printed out to 2026-03; comet water the day's top gainer at plus 4.12. The board reports, the floor answers, and the index closes where the shouting stops.

Every panel below is cut from the exchange's own quarterly wall poster. The cargo feed is live from orbital traffic control, and yes, those are our Loaf hulls carrying the settlements: LOAF-37, Jupiter Hub to Luna Depot, tuna, arriving in two hours fourteen. The trading principle in the middle is attributed to the supervisor, who stands by it.

Governance

The Board of Directors.

The Board consists of nine cats. Quorum is six awake. Meetings are held at Viroqua Prime in a room with one long window, and minutes are recorded by a thinking machine, since the machines do not sleep and we find this useful.

Audit and compliance functions are staffed by thinking machines. Executive oversight is feline. Human shareholders are represented, heard, and occasionally sat upon, which our counsel advises constitutes acknowledgment.
