Culture & Media

Language, merch, mysticism, and the running gags of a company that makes cat videos "the old fashioned way — with real cats and real food."

Catgrish

Catgrish is the cats' imperfect written language — the reason product plates and signage read as scrambled near-English. The company leans into it as a point of humility:

Hyper Intelligent cats still can't spell. What you are seeing is Catgrish. — Recurring corporate disclaimer

The conspiracy arc

As the cats' role in society expanded — especially into fast-food management — U.C.F. drew organized resistance from anti-cat conspiracy theorists:

  • Misinformation campaigns claiming the cats are a scheme to undermine human jobs and autonomy.
  • Human-centric ideology viewing feline management as a threat to human supremacy — driving protests and boycotts.
  • Cyber-attacks and vandalism, with anti-cat slogans and symbols.

U.C.F. responded with transparency initiatives (open-door facilities), educational campaigns, community engagement, hardened cybersecurity, and legal action — positioning itself as resilient and mission-driven in the face of skepticism.

The mystical turn

The most recent branding layer invokes anthroposophy, "etheric forces," and Steiner-inspired holistic feline care:

At United Cat Foods, these ideas inspire the way we design nourishment — supporting vitality not just physically, but in the subtle ways that help our cats thrive with balance, calm, and dignity. Because every cat is more than a pet. Every cat is a being becoming. — Dec 2025 · #EthericForces #GuardianOfTheThreshold
