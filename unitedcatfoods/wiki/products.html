Marketed through the story of Whiskers, an aging cat who — after a few sips — brightens, paws playfully at the bottle, and darts around the room "with a vivacity I hadn't seen in years." More than a drink, Crazy 5 is positioned as "an adventure in a bottle," promising enjoyment and vitality. Available at pet stores, online platforms, and select veterinary clinics.
