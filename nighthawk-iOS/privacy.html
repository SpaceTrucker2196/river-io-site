Nighthawk Tube

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 July 2026
◀ back to nighthawk
The short version: Nighthawk Tube collects nothing about you. There is no account, no sign-in, no network connection, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. The app makes sound and light on your device and keeps to itself.

Who this covers

This policy applies to the Nighthawk Tube iOS app (bundle identifier io.river.nighthawk), published by River. Contact: jeff@river.io.

Information we collect

None. Nighthawk Tube does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. Specifically, the app:

  • has no user accounts and asks for no name, email, or login;
  • makes no network requests and contains no server or cloud backend of its own;
  • includes no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs;
  • does not use the microphone, camera, contacts, photos, or location, and never reads any health data; it only outputs audio through your device's speakers or headphones (with one optional exception noted under Apple Health below);
  • does not employ Apple's App Tracking Transparency, because it tracks nothing.

What stays on your device

The app saves a small amount of information locally, on your device only, and never sends it anywhere:

  • Your unlock status, a single flag recording whether you've purchased Pro, so the app remembers it between launches;
  • Your last knob and timer settings, where applicable, so the instrument opens the way you left it.

This data lives in the app's private storage and is removed if you delete the app.

Apple Health (optional)

If you start a session timer, Nighthawk Tube can record the completed minutes to Apple Health as a Mindful Minutes entry, but only after you explicitly grant permission when the app asks. This is strictly write-only: the app logs finished sessions and never reads anything from your Health data. Those entries live in Apple's Health app under your own control, are never transmitted to us or to anyone else, and are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. You can decline the prompt, or revoke access at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Health, and the instrument works exactly the same without it.

Purchases

Nighthawk Tube offers one optional, non-consumable in-app purchase, Pro ($2.99), which unlocks the full session timer and the preset library. Payments are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. We never see or receive your payment details, card number, or Apple Account information. Apple's handling of that transaction is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. To restore a previous purchase, the app asks Apple to confirm your entitlement, again with no personal data passing to us.

Children

Nighthawk Tube is safe for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children. It contains no user-generated content, messaging, or external links to unmoderated spaces.

Data sharing & sale

Because we collect no personal data, there is nothing to share, sell, rent, or disclose to anyone, for any purpose, ever.

Your rights

Regulations such as the GDPR and CCPA grant rights to access, correct, or delete personal data held about you. We hold none, so there is nothing to request. Any settings the app stores locally are fully under your control and are erased by deleting the app.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects privacy, this page will be updated and the "last updated" date above revised. Material changes would be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy in Nighthawk Tube? Write to jeff@river.io.
