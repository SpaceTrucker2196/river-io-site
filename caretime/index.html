CareTime EVV

The timesheet that keeps up with you, not the other way around.

Made for the people doing the work

You're paid to care for someone — a parent, a child, a neighbor, a client. The hours you put in are how you cover rent, gas, groceries. CareTime EVV is built so those hours land on a clean, billable timesheet without you having to remember every minute at the end of a twelve-hour day.

No agency dashboard watching over your shoulder. No company collecting your hours behind your back. Just an app on your phone that knows you started bathing at 7:14 because that's when you tapped Bathing.

What it does for you

Tap. Switch. Stop. That's the whole interaction.

A grid of big buttons for the tasks you actually do — bathing, transfers, meal prep, meds, laundry, errands. Tap a task to start its timer. Tap a different task and the first one closes, the new one opens, in one motion. No clock-out screen. No menus to dig through.

Captures the visit, not your day.

At the start and end of each visit, CareTime EVV asks for one location fix — proof you were where you said you'd be (the EVV part). Once at the start. Once at the end. Never anywhere in between. Your phone doesn't track you, and neither do we.

One PDF at the end of the pay period.

When the pay period closes, the Export tab makes a timesheet PDF — date, start, stop, total, tasks completed, your signature, the participant's signature. Email it, AirDrop it, print it and fax it, hand-deliver it. Whatever your billing provider takes.

Stays with you.

Replacing your phone? Sign into your Apple ID or Google account on the new one and restore from your encrypted device backup — your CareTime history comes back with the rest of your apps. We don't have a server. There's nothing to log in to.

What CareTime EVV doesn't do

If you handle private information about a person's care, the last thing you need is one more app collecting it. CareTime EVV doesn't.

  • No account to sign up for. No password to remember.
  • No analytics, no ads, no third-party trackers.
  • No background location. Two location fixes per visit — start and end — and that's all.
  • No selling your data. There's nothing to sell because we never receive it.
  • No corporate dashboard. Your hours are yours.

What is EVV?

Electronic Visit Verification is a federal requirement (the 21st Century Cures Act) for most Medicaid-funded home and community-based services. The rule says: when a paid caregiver provides a visit, the system has to log when the visit started, when it ended, where it happened, what service was performed, who provided it, and who received it.

Your billing provider almost certainly tracks this already, through whatever app or paper system they ask you to use. CareTime EVV doesn't replace that. It gives you your own complete record, in your own pocket, in a timesheet you can actually read.
