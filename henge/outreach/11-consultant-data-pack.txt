4. MAC: NATIVE BUILD OR IPAD APP ON MAC?
=======================================================================

  Both exist, and this is the part worth reading twice.

  On the App Store, the Mac presence is the iOS app running on Apple
  silicon. "Make this app available on Apple silicon Macs" is checked,
  minimum set to Automatic (macOS 14.0). App Store Connect currently
  shows "This app's compatibility with Apple silicon Macs hasn't been
  verified yet", which is the flag you spotted. There is a Verify
  Compatibility action available and it has not been used.

  Separately there is a genuine native macOS build, a distinct target in
  the project, distributed as a direct .dmg from river.io/henge. That
  build is v0.1.0 dated 3 August, it is behind the 0.2.0 App Store
  build, and it is not notarized, so first launch needs right-click Open.

  So: Mac App Store = iPad app on Mac, unverified. Native Mac = direct
  download only, and stale.
