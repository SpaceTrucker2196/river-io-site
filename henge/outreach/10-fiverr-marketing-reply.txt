On measurement, partly agree. App Store Connect already gives me app units, IAP
units and proceeds, so my aggregate download-to-purchase rate is measurable
now, for free. Apple Ads reports taps, installs and cost per install per
keyword natively through Apple's own attribution, with no SDK. What I genuinely
cannot get is purchases attributed back to a specific keyword or campaign.
That is a real gap and it is the one that matters for optimisation, so you are
right that it constrains what Meta can do.

But I am not adding an attribution SDK. No network, no account, nothing
collected is the product, not a setting. It is in the privacy label, the
marketing and the architecture, and the app ships with zero third-party
dependencies by design. Instrumenting it to measure a channel that may not pay
back is trading the differentiator for the measurement.

needs about 19 percent, which is not. So the entire ads question reduces to one
measurable thing: what cost per tap actually comes in at on the uncontested
set.
