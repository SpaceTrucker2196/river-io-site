Henge for Mac

The early build — the whole instrument, still on the workbench.

DOWNLOAD HENGE.DMG

v0.1.0 · 8.7M · UNIVERSAL · macOS 14+

To install: open the image and drag Henge into Applications.

First launch: this early build is not yet notarized with Apple, so macOS will warn you. Right-click the app, choose Open, and confirm once — after that it opens normally. When the app ships properly it will carry a Developer ID signature and this paragraph will be deleted with some ceremony.

What you get: the working almanac — the computed sky over the surveyed stones, the time scrubber to 100,000×, both monument states and the four-thousand-year passage between them. It is a build in progress, and it behaves like one in places.

No network, no telemetry, no account. The almanac works in a field with no signal.
The astronomy, the archaeology and the accuracy figures live on the research site.
